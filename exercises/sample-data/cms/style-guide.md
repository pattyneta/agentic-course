# Content Style Guide — Horizon Travel Group CMS

All product copy published to the CMS must follow these rules. This guide is
deliberately long-ish: in the Day 1 stretch goal you cache it as a stable
prompt prefix.

## Voice and tone

- Warm, knowledgeable, and plain-spoken — like a well-travelled friend, not a brochure.
- British English throughout (organise, colour, travelled).
- Second person ("you'll sail into Valletta at sunrise"), never first person plural sales-speak ("we are delighted to offer").
- No exclamation marks. No ALL CAPS. No "ULTRA", "AMAZING", "once-in-a-lifetime".

## Structure

- **Title**: max 60 characters, no ship name, no price. Lead with the experience, not the product code.
- **Summary**: one paragraph, 40–70 words, mentions region, duration, and one concrete highlight.
- **Body**: 2–4 short paragraphs. First covers the journey arc (where you start, standout ports, where you finish). Later paragraphs cover ship life and practicalities.
- **SEO meta description**: max 155 characters, includes the region and duration, ends with a soft call to action.

## Facts and compliance

- Prices: always "from £X per person" — never bare numbers, never USD unless quoting an onboard charge, in which case say "payable on board in US dollars".
- Always state what is NOT included if the supplier copy mentions it (gratuities, flights).
- Never invent facts not present in the supplier copy. If a detail is missing, omit it.
- Dates: "September 2026", not "SEP 26" or "09/26".
- Port names: "Rome (Civitavecchia)" style — city first, port in brackets.

## Tags

- Choose 3–6 tags from: `mediterranean`, `caribbean`, `northern-europe`, `atlantic-islands`, `family`, `adults-only`, `luxury`, `budget`, `fly-cruise`, `no-fly`, `short-break`, `winter-sun`.
- Tags are lowercase kebab-case. Do not invent new tags.
